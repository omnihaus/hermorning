Privacy Policy

Your mornings are yours. Her Morning is built so that what you tell it stays on your iPhone.

What Her Morning keeps

Your first name, your alarms and settings, your morning check-ins (how you felt and the word you chose), and, if you turn it on, the start date and length of your period cycle.

Where it's kept

Only on your iPhone, inside Her Morning. There are no Her Morning accounts and no Her Morning servers, so nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we never do

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no selling or sharing of your data. Your cycle and mood information never leaves your phone.

Purchases

Bloom+ is bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment, and Her Morning never sees your card or Apple Account details. Her Morning only learns whether Bloom+ is active.

Alarms and reminders

Alarms and the bedtime reminder are created on your iPhone by iOS. They aren't sent through the internet.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to You, then Delete all my data. Everything Her Morning keeps is erased from your iPhone straight away. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Her Morning does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
