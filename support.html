My alarm didn't ring

Open Her Morning and make sure alarms are allowed (You, then Alarms). If you turned it off, go to the iPhone Settings app, then Her Morning, and turn on Alarms. Her Morning alarms ring even on Silent and in a Focus.

How do I turn off the alarm?

Tap Start my morning or slide to stop on the Lock Screen. Her Morning opens and the alarm keeps playing until you finish your way to rise, for example holding the flower until it blooms. If you leave without finishing, a gentle backup alarm rings a few minutes later.

The alarm is quiet

On the Lock Screen, the alarm uses your iPhone's ringer volume. Inside the app it uses the media volume. Turn both up in Control Center or with the volume buttons.

Restoring Bloom+

In Her Morning, go to You, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Bloom+ with.

Cancelling Bloom+

In Her Morning, go to You, then Manage or cancel subscription, or open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. You keep the free version of Her Morning.
